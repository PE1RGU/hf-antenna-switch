Purpose: one record of every design choice with its reason, the rejected options, assumptions, calculations, standards and measurements. The detailed design document is drafted from this log. Newest entries at the end of each section. Rev C summary: docs/architecture.md; requirements: docs/requirements.md. Archived records: archive/ (list in archive/README.md).

Status keys: decided / assumed / to confirm / open / superseded.

## 7. Sources and documents

Paths are relative to the master folder (the local repository). The claude.ai Project docs (claude/...) are a mirror.

- Requirements and architecture: docs/requirements.md, docs/architecture.md (rev C), docs/design/ (one document per board), docs/test/test-spec.md.
- Archive: archive/README.md lists every archived item and what replaced it (architecture v0.1: archive/architecture-v0.1-2026-09-09.md; rev A card documents and site plan rev B: archive/block-diagram-rev-a-2026-09/; rev A/B card cage and backplane: archive/rev-b-backplane-2026-09/).
- Datasheets and register: Datasheets/_register.md. The PDFs stay in Datasheets/ and are not in the repository.
- Schematics (each generator runs from its own folder):
  - RF board: hardware/unit/rf-board/ (rf-board.kicad_pro, gen_rfboard_sch.py, _sym/, rf-board.pretty/, rf-board.pdf).
  - Controller board: hardware/unit/controller-board/ (controller-board.kicad_pro, gen_controller_sch.py, _sym/, lib/controller-board.pretty/, controller-board.pdf).
  - Shack interface: hardware/shack-interface/ (shack-interface.kicad_pro, gen_shack_sch.py, _sym/, lib/shack-interface.pretty/, shack-interface-rev-a.md).
- Footprint generators: hardware/shack-interface/lib/ (gen_photorelay_fp.py, gen_minidin_fp.py, gen_devkit_fp.py). The footprints are used in lib/shack-interface.pretty/ and, unchanged, in hardware/unit/controller-board/lib/controller-board.pretty/. RF board: hardware/unit/rf-board/gen_so239_fp.py writes the SO-239 strap footprints into rf-board.pretty/ (D-105).

- Diagrams: hardware/docs/diagrams/ (gen_diagrams.py, SVG and PNG).
