Antenna Port Card

One card per antenna line. It does three things: connects the antenna to its matrix line only when told to and only while the controller is alive, grounds it otherwise, and tells the controller what it is and whether RF is present. Same footprint and backplane pinout as the Radio Port card. Parent diagram: Shack Relay Matrix.

Blocks

BlockFunction and choice
K1 SPDT relayThe only moving part in the RF path. COM to the antenna, NO to the matrix line, NC to ground through the static bleed. De-energised is grounded, so a dead controller, released /RLY_EN or lost 12 V all leave the antenna parked. 12 V coil, 16 A HF-rated signal relay class (RT2/Omron G2RL or similar), rated well beyond 100 W at 50 Ω.
Static bleed1 MΩ in parallel with an RF choke on the NC side. Drains precipitation static while grounded; the choke keeps the DC path when the bias-T option is fitted.
GDT90 V gas-discharge arrestor from antenna centre to chassis, on the antenna side of K1 so it protects the relay too. Not a lightning solution; that stays at the mast base and the shed entry.
RF detectorCapacitive tap after the matrix connector, Schottky detector, comparator with fixed threshold. Gives RF_DET to the expander and pulls /INT. Route verification: RF seen on a card the controller thinks is idle, or on an RX-only card, is a fault and drops /RLY_EN.
Coupler footprintUnpopulated on rev A. Directional coupler plus dual detector lands here; fwd/ref analog goes to the header for a later ADC card or controller input. Per-antenna SWR without a new PCB.
Bias-T optionSeries DC-block capacitor on the matrix side and an inductor injecting 12 V on the antenna side. Fitted only on a card feeding an active RX antenna. Enable via expander output, flag in EEPROM.
I2C expanderPCA9554 class, address from SLOT_ID. Inputs: RF_DET, DIP switches. Outputs: ROUTE, bias-T enable, LED.
ID EEPROM24C02 class, address from SLOT_ID. Holds antenna name, RX-only flag, bias-T flag, card revision. Controller reads it at boot, so the card carries its identity to any slot.
DIP overrideTwo switches: force RX-only, force bias-T off. Hardware truth beats EEPROM when they disagree; controller logs the mismatch.
Relay driverLogic-level MOSFET with flyback diode. Gate is ROUTE AND /RLY_EN in hardware (one gate or a driver with an enable pin). Firmware alone cannot energise K1 when the watchdog has released the bus.
Card powerPolyfuse on +12V RLY, LC filter so relay switching does not appear on the RF ground, local 3V3 decoupling. One card failing short does not take the rail down.

RX-only variant

Same PCB. EEPROM flag and DIP set RX-only; bias-T parts fitted if the antenna is active. The controller refuses this card on any TX path. The detector is the hardware tripwire behind that rule: if it ever sees transmit-level RF, the whole matrix goes to safe state.

Open items

  1. Relay part: confirm contact rating and isolation at 30 MHz for 100 W with the NC side grounded; check hot-switch survival for the fault case.
  2. Detector threshold: set between the strongest expected receive signal and the lowest transmit power that matters, roughly 0 dBm to +20 dBm at the tap.
  3. Whether the coupler footprint uses a Tandem Match (two ferrite transformers) or a stripline coupler; footprint size differs.
  4. Card edge: 100 × 80 mm Eurocard-half with the SO-239 on the front edge, or a rear-panel bulkhead SO-239 with a short coax jumper to the card.
